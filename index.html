Algorithms & data structures 2026 · Preprint

Linear Probing with
Non-Greedy Insertions

Andrew Krapivin, William Kuszmaul, Yixuan Wang

A non-greedy approach to linear probing that improves worst-case expected insertion time without reordering stored elements. The work introduces interlinear probing and establishes a lower bound for stable insertion strategies.

Research details +

For a table filled to 1 − 1/x, interlinear probing reduces worst-case expected insertion time from Θ(x²) to O(x log x), while retaining O(x) amortized insertion time. Negative queries take O(x (log x)²) worst-case expected time. A lower bound of Ω(x √log x) constrains every stable insertion strategy.

Complexity & zero knowledge Unpublished manuscript

Perfect Zero-Knowledge
PCPs with Low Error

Yixuan Wang, Guy Weissenberg, Burcu Yıldız

Perfect zero-knowledge probabilistically checkable proofs for NP with low soundness error. The work studies how to preserve exact zero knowledge while reducing error and balancing proof length, query complexity, and alphabet size.

Research details +

We construct polynomial-length PZK PCPs with inverse-polynomial soundness error, poly(log log n) queries, and a subpolynomial alphabet, based on Dinur-Harsha PCPs. It also gives a two-query construction with asymmetric answer lengths, proof-length lower bounds, and applications to zero-knowledge arguments.
