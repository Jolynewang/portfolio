Bio

I am a senior (fourth-year) undergraduate student at Carnegie Mellon University, with a primary major in computer science, and a double major in mathematical sciences.

Living in places from Australia to Switzerland, the raw beauty of the nature always fascinates me. I aspire to document what I have the opportunity to witness with my drone.

Academically, I am interested in theoretical computer science and complexity theory broadly. My current project is on perfect zero knowledge Probabilistically Checkable Proofs for NTIME.
