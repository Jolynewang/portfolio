I’m Yixuan Wang, a PhD student at the University of Cambridge, exploring the limits of computation.

I am supervised by Prof. Tom Gur and supported by the Cambridge Australia Poynton Scholarship through the Cambridge Trust.

Research & background

I am broadly interested in complexity theory, particularly perfect zero-knowledge proofs and probabilistically checkable proofs. My completed projects include Linear Probing with Non-Greedy Insertions and the unpublished manuscript Perfect Zero-Knowledge PCPs with Low Error.

Previously, I studied computer science and mathematical sciences at Carnegie Mellon University. My research at EPFL, supervised by Prof. Alessandro Chiesa, explored perfect zero-knowledge probabilistically checkable proofs for NTIME and for NP with low soundness error.

Beyond the proofs

Having lived in places from Australia to Switzerland, the raw beauty of nature has always fascinated me. I attempt to document what I have the opportunity to witness through the lens of my drone.

My films bring together documentary and editorial cinematography, with selected works from Britanniahütte, Seven Sisters, Bushranger Bay, and Ticino.
