About

Short bio.

I am Yixuan (Jolyne) Wang. I am a first-year PhD student at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Prof. Tom Gur and supported by the Cambridge Australia Poynton Scholarship through the Cambridge Trust. Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies in computer science and mathematical sciences at Carnegie Mellon University. My research interests broadly lie in algorithms and complexity theory; more specifically, I have worked on randomised algorithms and probabilistic proof systems.

Beyond the proofs.

AI can produce theorems faster than researchers, and after all, we are not machines that turn coffee into theorems. Outside of work, I enjoy classical literature and the outdoors. The beauty of mountains always leaves me in awe, and I attempt to capture them through drone films and photos. Sometimes I write blogs about random thoughts and experiences.
